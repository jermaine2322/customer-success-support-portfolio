## About the examples

The onboarding, knowledge-base, support-case, office-hours, service-operations, and feature-request materials are demonstration work created for this portfolio. They describe **Sample Planning Workspace**, a fictional browser-based application for reviewing consumer-goods planning data. Its controls, roles, records, errors, tickets, and outcomes are invented to make the examples concrete. These are not Sciemo product instructions or records of customer engagements I performed.

The [case studies](technology-adoption-and-migration.md) describe my actual experience and distinguish my contribution from the wider team's work. No real customer, patient, police, military operational, or employer system records are included.

## How I approach support

I start with the user's goal and the impact of the problem. I document what I can verify, explain the next step, and keep ownership of customer communication while the appropriate technical team investigates. A request is ready to close when the outcome has been checked and the user knows what changed. Repeated questions should also inform a guide, a training exercise, or a specific product improvement.
