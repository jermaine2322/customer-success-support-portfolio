# Running useful client office hours

**Demonstration session plan** for Sample Planning Workspace. The session and participants are hypothetical.

## Purpose and preparation

Run a 30-minute session for one customer team. Ask its contact for the top two questions in advance. Review open requests, the team's approved data scope, and recent product changes. Invite a technical specialist when a known question needs one, while keeping one facilitator responsible for the session.

Use a test workspace when demonstrating changes. Confirm permission before recording or sharing customer-specific material.

## Session plan

| Time | Activity | Evidence of progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 minutes | Confirm the team's goal and prioritize questions | Participants agree on the two tasks to address |
| 5–15 minutes | Demonstrate one task using approved example data | Users can explain the steps and active filters |
| 15–23 minutes | Ask a participant to repeat the task | The participant completes it or identifies the blocking step |
| 23–27 minutes | Address questions and capture unresolved issues | Each item has a category and an owner |
| 27–30 minutes | Recap actions and next updates | Participants know what happens next and when |

## Facilitation example

Ask, “Which decision are you trying to make with this view?” before demonstrating controls. Explain one step at a time. Afterward, ask the participant to show the task back rather than asking only, “Does that make sense?”

If a problem requires investigation, capture the scope and expected result, create a support record, and agree on the next update. Avoid letting one unresolved issue consume the whole session.

## Follow-up example

“Today we practiced selecting the right planning scenario and checking report filters. Your designated planner completed the walkthrough independently. We still need to investigate the summary/export mismatch; it is tracked as CASE-001 with support as the communication owner. Your next update is at 3:00 p.m. Eastern. Before our next session, please try the same workflow with one authorized product group and note any step that is unclear.”

## Improve the next session

Record the task attempted, where help was needed, and whether the participant completed it independently. Repeated confusion should prompt a guide revision or a targeted exercise. Review these observations alongside unresolved issues and customer feedback; attendance alone does not establish adoption.
